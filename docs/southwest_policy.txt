Southwest Airlines Baggage Policy:
Southwest Airlines allows two free checked bags per passenger, each weighing up to 50 pounds and measuring up to 62 inches (length + width + height combined).

Southwest Airlines Boarding Policy:
Southwest does not assign seats. Passengers board in groups (A, B, C) based on check-in time. Check-in opens 24 hours before departure.

Southwest Airlines Cancellation Policy:
Southwest does not charge change or cancellation fees on any fare. Funds from a canceled flight can be used for future travel.
